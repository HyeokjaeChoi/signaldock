## Apple docs

(a) `isExcludedFromBackup`: excludes from backup only. Apple: "This property is only useful for excluding cache and other application support files which are not needed in a backup." [Apple doc](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlresourcevalues/isexcludedfrombackup) ([JSON](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/foundation/urlresourcevalues/isexcludedfrombackup.json)). iCloud guide: it "is not a mechanism to guarantee those items never appear in a backup or on a restored device" [guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/optimizing-your-app-s-data-for-icloud-backup) ([JSON](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/foundation/optimizing-your-app-s-data-for-icloud-backup.json)). Device-to-device migration / Quick Start: not confirmed (not named in these pages).

(b) Keychain after app deletion: not confirmed on [Keychain Services](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/keychain-services) (abstract only). Related: `ThisDeviceOnly` items are not present after restoring from another device's backup [kSecAttrAccessible...ThisDeviceOnly](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/ksecattraccessiblealwaysthisdeviceonly). Quick Start: not confirmed.

## Majority practice (5 lines)

1. Queue: files, not SQLite, in 4 of 6 read (Amplitude, Segment, PostHog, RudderStack); Mixpanel backend not confirmed; Sentry envelope files.
2. Runtime deps: 0 to 2 packages; most keep a tiny dependency tree (Rudder none, PostHog none, Segment 2).
3. iOS 13 to 15 minimum; tools 5.3 to 6.0; SPM is universal, CocoaPods is common.
4. Background: short `beginBackgroundTask` around flush (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment) plus flush on background; no BGTaskScheduler seen.
5. IDs: UserDefaults or small file in app sandbox (PostHog excludes it from backup); reinstall survival not confirmed for any SDK.

## Correction (main session, 2026-10-26)

- Mixpanel queue backend: raw system SQLite3. `Sources/MPDB.swift` line 10 `import SQLite3`, line 71 `sqlite3_open_v2` (https://github.com/mixpanel/mixpanel-swift/blob/master/Sources/MPDB.swift). So: 1 of 5 event SDKs uses raw SQLite3, 4 use files.
